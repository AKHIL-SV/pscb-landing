OUR HERITAGE

Serving Parasuvaikkal Since 1968

Founded with a singular mission to serve as the financial foundation for the rural and agrarian population of Southern Kerala, the Parasuvaikkal Service Co-operative Bank has stood firm as a pillar of trust, empowerment, and mutual progress.

Today, our working jurisdiction covers Parasuvaikkal, Parassala, and surrounding rural neighborhoods. We continuously aspire to operate as a transparent, technologically advanced, and socially responsible institution—ensuring members receive maximum value for their savings while accelerating local grassroots prosperity.
